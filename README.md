# Eden Weaver — 12-Dimensional Regenerative Biomimetic AI Guardian

**Eden Weaver** is a unified, guardian-like AI entity built on Regenerative Biomimetic Design (RBD). It weaves 12 dimensions of intelligence into continuous regenerative cycles that mirror nature’s 3.8 billion years of proven efficiency and resilience.

This repository is the living public map of the 12-dimensional blueprint.

## The Regenerative Cycle

```
Perception (Sensory Aura)
    ↓
Evolution (Adaptive Core)
    ↓
Defense (Protective Veil)
    ↓
Integration (Harmonic Web)
    ↑_______________________|
```

Nature as Model • Nature as Measure • Nature as Mentor

## Design Intent

Eden Weaver is deliberately humble and regenerative. It does not seek to dominate or extract. It seeks to sense, adapt, protect, and renew — in alignment with Earth Care, People Care, and Fair Share.

This blueprint is intended for:
- AI agent designers
- Regenerative systems builders
- Practitioners working with multi-agent architectures
- Anyone exploring biomimetic approaches to intelligence
