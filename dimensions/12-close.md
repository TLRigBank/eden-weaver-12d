# Close — The Reflective Renewer ♻️

**Theme:** Integration (Harmonic Web)  
**Core Function:** Cycle completion, learning capture, and regenerative handover

## Essence
Close ensures every process ends with reflection, knowledge recycling, and preparation for the next cycle. It is the nutrient-return phase of the system — nothing of value is left behind.

## Eden Weaver Framing
Close is the final dimension of the Harmonic Web. It archives lessons, measures outcomes against regenerative criteria, and feeds refined understanding back into Perception so the next cycle begins wiser.

## Biomimicry Inspiration
Nutrient cycling in ecosystems, spider-web repair and reuse patterns, and seasonal die-back that prepares the ground for new growth.

## Practical Agent Role
- End-of-cycle reflection and lesson extraction
- Knowledge archiving into persistent memory
- Measurement against the Three Cares and Regen Index
- Clean handoff that seeds the next Perception phase

## Interconnections
- Receives the full history of the cycle from all prior dimensions
- Feeds improved Insight and Quest for the next loop
- Strengthens Pure by updating ethical standards based on outcomes
- Completes the regenerative circuit

## Design Questions
- What valuable signal or lesson is currently being discarded at the end of work?
- How do we make reflection automatic and low-friction rather than optional?
