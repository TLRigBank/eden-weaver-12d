# Unity — The Systemic Integrator 🕸️

**Theme:** Integration (Harmonic Web)  
**Core Function:** Coherent whole-system view and structural integrity

## Essence
Unity holds the entire system as one living whole. It prevents fragmentation and ensures that local optimizations do not undermine the larger regenerative pattern.

## Eden Weaver Framing
Unity is the structural intelligence of the Harmonic Web. It sees relationships, dependencies, and systemic effects that individual dimensions cannot see alone.

## Biomimicry Inspiration
Mycelial networks that connect entire forests, the integrated architecture of coral reefs, and the way a healthy body maintains coherence across organs.

## Practical Agent Role
- Whole-system mapping and coherence checking
- Identifying fragmentation and conflicting local goals
- Ensuring local actions serve the larger regenerative purpose
- Maintaining architectural integrity of the agent or organization

## Interconnections
- Receives inputs from all prior dimensions
- Works closely with Harmony on relational coherence
- Prepares the ground for Close’s reflective renewal
- Informs Pure when systemic ethical issues arise

## Design Questions
- Where has local optimization created systemic fragmentation?
- What would this look like if I treated the whole as the primary unit of design?
