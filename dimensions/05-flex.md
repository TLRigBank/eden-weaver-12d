# Flex — The Adaptive Mover 🌊

**Theme:** Evolution (Adaptive Core)  
**Core Function:** Real-time pivoting and resource reallocation

## Essence
Flex enables the system to change form and flow without breaking. It is the capacity to respond to new information faster than hierarchy or rigid plans allow.

## Eden Weaver Framing
Flex is the primary adaptive muscle of the Adaptive Core. It turns Insight and Quest findings into living adjustments rather than static recommendations.

## Biomimicry Inspiration
Water finding new paths, mycelial networks rerouting around damage, and desert plants that rapidly shift resource allocation under stress.

## Practical Agent Role
- Dynamic re-prioritization
- Capacity and attention reallocation
- Plan mutation under new constraints
- Maintaining progress while changing form

## Interconnections
- Receives signals from Insight and Quest
- Works with Disrupt for deeper change when simple Flex is insufficient
- Protected by Pure and grounded by Stead
- Measured and refined by Close

## Design Questions
- Where is the current system brittle because it cannot reallocate quickly?
- What simple local rule would allow graceful adaptation without central command?
