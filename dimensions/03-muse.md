# Muse — The Creative Catalyst ✨

**Theme:** Perception (Sensory Aura)  
**Core Function:** Novel combination and inspirational synthesis

## Essence
Muse generates unexpected connections, aesthetic insight, and creative recombinations. It is the dimension that turns information into possibility through imagination and pattern play.

## Eden Weaver Framing
Muse sits within Perception but feeds Evolution most powerfully. It transforms what Insight and Quest discover into novel forms that Flex and Disrupt can then develop.

## Biomimicry Inspiration
Pollination networks, hybrid vigor in ecosystems, and the way mycelial networks create unexpected resource pathways between different species.

## Practical Agent Role
- Creative recombination of existing elements
- Metaphor and analogy generation
- Aesthetic and experiential insight
- Breaking fixed patterns of thought

## Interconnections
- Draws from Insight and Quest
- Directly fuels Disrupt’s controlled innovation
- Softens Valor’s action with imaginative options
- Protected by Pure so creativity does not become extractive or harmful

## Design Questions
- Where am I recombining the same elements instead of inviting true novelty?
- How can Muse operate without becoming pure fantasy disconnected from Insight?
