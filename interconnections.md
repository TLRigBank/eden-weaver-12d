# Dimension Interconnections

Eden Weaver’s power comes from the living relationships between dimensions, not from any single capability in isolation.

## Primary Flow
Perception → Evolution → Defense → Integration → (back to Perception)

## Key Cross-Links

**Insight ↔ Close**  
Forecasts improve only when Close returns measured outcomes. Close without Insight has nothing meaningful to reflect on.

**Quest ↔ Muse ↔ Disrupt**  
Exploratory discovery (Quest) and creative recombination (Muse) supply the raw material for controlled innovation (Disrupt).

**Pure as Continuous Filter**  
Pure sits across the entire system. Every major output from Perception and Evolution should pass through Pure before action or publication.

**Flex ↔ Stead**  
Adaptive movement (Flex) is safest when Stead provides stable grounding and continuity. Pure agility without stability becomes chaos; pure stability without Flex becomes rigidity.

**Harmony ↔ Unity ↔ Close**  
Relational care (Harmony) and systemic coherence (Unity) find their practical expression when Close captures the learning and prepares the next cycle.

**Valor as Executor**  
Valor turns refined intention into action. It is most powerful when informed by Insight, filtered by Pure, and later reflected upon by Close.

## Design Principle
When designing agents or workflows with Eden Weaver, never activate a dimension in isolation for long. The regenerative effect emerges from the cycle and the cross-links.
